# Garage Gadget — quick start

## 1. Power it on

The light on the board tells you what it is doing.

| Light | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Blue**, slowly breathing | Waiting to be set up | Go to step 2 |
| **Green**, steady | Working. Door closed | Nothing |
| **Amber / red**, flickering | Door is open | Nothing |
| **Red**, fast flashing | Alarm | Check the door |
| **Magenta**, slow flashing | Lost Wi-Fi | It keeps retrying by itself |
| **White**, steady | Starting up | Wait a few seconds |

## 2. Set it up

On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network **GarageGadget-XXXX**, password
**garagesetup**.

The setup page usually opens by itself. If it does not, open
**http://192.168.4.1** in your browser.

Work through the five steps, then rejoin your normal Wi-Fi and open the

## 3. Let it move the door

**A new unit will not open your door, on purpose.** Once it is mounted and
the door state on screen matches the real door, turn **Door control** on at
the top of the **Control** tab.

## Forgot the password?

Hold the recovery button for **10 seconds** while the unit is running. It
reopens its own Wi-Fi network so you can set a new password. Nothing else is
erased — your settings, history and calibration all survive.

---

**Everything else is on the device.** Notifications, history, alarms, door
calibration and Home Assistant are all explained in the **Help** tab of the
dashboard, with no internet connection needed.
